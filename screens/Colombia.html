Experience the heartbeat of South America. From Andean peaks to Caribbean rhythms, discover a land of infinite flavor and warmth.

The Survival Guide

Culture Shocks

Navigating the beautiful complexities of Colombian daily life. Don't just visit—belong.

01

Hora Latina

Punctuality is flexible. "Ya casi llego" (I'm almost there) could mean anything from 5 minutes to half an hour. Relax and enjoy the flow.

schedule
02

Warm Greetings

Handshakes are formal; a light kiss on the right cheek or a vigorous hug is the standard among friends and acquaintances.

handshake
03

Personal Space

Colombians are tactile. Expect closer standing distances during conversation and plenty of friendly physical contact.

groups
04

Coffee Culture

Don't expect "Tinto" to be a fancy latte. It's small, black, and sweet—the social fuel of every street corner and office.

coffee
05

Safety: No dar papaya

"Don't give papaya"—a local proverb meaning don't make yourself an easy target. Stay aware, avoid flashy jewelry in crowded areas, and use common sense.
