Etiquette

Physical contact

Brazilians are famously warm. Be prepared for hugs, air kisses on both cheeks, and touches on the arm during conversation.

Daily Life

Late dinners

Standard dinner times range from 8 PM to 10 PM. Most restaurants only start getting lively after the sun goes down.

celebration
Social Spirit

Carnival energy

It's not just a week in February; it's a year-round mindset of celebration, music, and spontaneous outdoor gatherings.

chat_bubble
Communication

Directness

While polite, Brazilians are remarkably honest about feelings and personal questions. Expect refreshing transparency.

thumb_up

Thumbs up usage

The "Joinha" is the universal remote for Brazil. It means yes, thank you, okay, I understand, or just hello. Master this gesture and you've mastered 50% of the language.
